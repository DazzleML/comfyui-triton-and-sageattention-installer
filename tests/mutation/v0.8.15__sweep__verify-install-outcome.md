## Anomalies (resolved)

First sweep run: 8 apply-failures, all multi-line originals. Root cause: the target file is CRLF (5,979 CRLF, 0 bare LF) and the spec's `\n` originals matched nothing. Fixed by normalizing the spec's originals/mutateds to `\r\n`; single-line mutants were unaffected, which is why 4 applied on round one. No false verdicts resulted — the failures were loud, not silent. **Lesson for future sweeps in this repo: normalize spec line endings to the target file's before running.**

Restore-verify after both sweeps: `git status --short` identical to pre-sweep capture except the new `tests/mutation/` artifacts; full suite green (316 passed, 2 xfailed).

## Refinement decision

One killable survivor triggered the loop-close (test written immediately, kill demonstrated). A second *generation* round was not run: the 12 mutants already covered every branch of the check matrix, both fences, the gate, the counter, and the scope map, and the sole survivor traced to a test-shape gap (now closed), not a generator blind spot. Stopping at one generation round, recorded here as a judgment call.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing — and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Specifically out of scope: the verifier's known blind spot (same-version churn passes by design, documented in its docstring) and the wiring of `_verify_install_outcome` into `install()` (covered separately by `test_install_actually_calls_the_verifier`).
